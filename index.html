Questions

Pricing, payment and practicalities

Do your prices include VAT?

No. All prices are shown excluding VAT. If VAT applies, it is added at the standard rate and shown clearly on your quote and invoice.

How do deposits and payments work?

Projects under £5,000 need a 50% deposit, with the balance on completion. Larger projects are split 40% / 30% / 30% across agreed milestones. Invoices are due within 14 days.

How are monthly plans billed?

Monthly plans are invoiced in advance and paid by Direct Debit. There's a 3-month minimum, then you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

What if I need something that isn't in a package?

We scope it with you and quote a fixed price before work starts, based on our £450 day rate. Changes to an agreed scope are always quoted separately first.

Can you deliver faster than the stated timeline?

Often, yes. Rush delivery is available for an extra 25%, depending on our schedule.

Who owns the website, workflows and code?

You do, once paid. Tool subscriptions and hosting sit on your own accounts wherever possible, so you're never locked in.

Where do you work?

Remote work, websites and automations are available anywhere in the UK. On-site POS installs and support cover Greater Manchester and up to 30 miles around it.

Do you handle our data safely?

Yes. We sign a data processing agreement with every client whose personal data we work with, use two-factor authentication throughout and follow UK GDPR.

What we collect
The details you enter in our enquiry form: your name, business, contact details and what you tell us about your project. We use them only to reply to you and prepare a quote. Our legal basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.
